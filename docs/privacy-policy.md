# Status Effects privacy policy

**Draft, not yet published.** Effective date: [add the date it goes live]

Status Effects ("the app") is a drink tracker that estimates how alcohol affects you. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **everything stays on your phone. We don't collect it, see it, sell it or share it.**

## What the app stores

To work out its estimates, the app keeps the following on your device:

- **Your character sheet:** weight, height, age, the body-water formula you chose (male or female), your usual bedtime and wake time, and your region for drinking guidelines.
- **Your drinking log:** each drink, water and food you log, with its time, and any prices you enter.
- **Your check-ins:** how you felt the morning after, and your answers to the drinking check-in questions in History.
- **Your saved drinks and settings,** such as theme, units and whether calories are shown.

## Where it's kept

All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it doesn't send your information to us or anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it doesn't use the internet at all.

If your phone backs up its apps (for example, iCloud Backup), the app's data may be included in that backup. Your backup is managed by Apple under your own account and Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

## What we can see

Nothing. We have no server and receive no data from the app, so we can't see, recover or delete your information for you.

## Deleting your data

- **Clear your history:** Options › Your data › Clear all history deletes every night, check-in and achievement. Your character sheet, settings and saved drinks are kept.
- **Delete everything:** uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.

## Health information

Drinking and body information is sensitive. Because it never leaves your phone, it isn't shared with insurers, employers, advertisers or anyone else. The app's numbers are estimates, not medical advice or a breathalyser reading, and never tell you whether you're safe to drive.

## Age

The app is meant only for people of legal drinking age where they live.

## Changes

If a future version changes what the app does with your information (for example, adding optional backups or sharing), we'll update this policy and say so in the app's release notes before the change takes effect.
